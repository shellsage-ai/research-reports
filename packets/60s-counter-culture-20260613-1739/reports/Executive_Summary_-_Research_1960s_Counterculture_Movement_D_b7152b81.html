The 1960s Counterculture was not a single organization or doctrine, but a loose and often unstable constellation of youth, artistic, political, spiritual, and lifestyle movements that challenged mainstream American authority, consumerism, racial hierarchy, sexual norms, Cold War militarism, and inherited ideas of respectability.
